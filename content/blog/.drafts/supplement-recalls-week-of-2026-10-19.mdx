## New recall announcements this week

Company recall announcements that FDA posted on its [recalls page](https://www.fda.gov/safety/recalls-market-withdrawals-safety-alerts) this week.

### Ultimate Forza Male Supplement (Tonight We Ride, LLC)

- **Company:** Tonight We Ride, LLC
- **Brand:** Ultimate Forza
- **Product:** Male Supplement
- **Reason (FDA summary):** “Product contains undeclared Sildenafil, Tadalafil, and Dapoxetine”
- **Products, lots and identifiers in the notice:**
  - “October 24, 2026, Tonight We Ride, LLC of Lakeville, Minnesota is voluntarily recalling all lots of Ultimate Forza Male Supplement capsules to the consumer level because it has been found to contain undeclared Sildenafil, Tadalafil, and Dapoxetine.”
  - “Ultimate Forza products subject to the recall include the Ultimate Forza Multi-Capsule/Box (Item \#810208, UPC 868592000002) and the Ultimate Forza Single Pack (Item \#810222, UPC 868592000040).”
  - “The Multi-Capsule/Box configuration contains ten blue hard-shell capsules, and the Single Pack contains one capsule.”
- **Where it was sold:**
  - “These items were distributed through certain independently owned and operated GNC franchise locations in the United States from 2017 to October 1, 2026.”
  - “The products were not sold through GNC corporate stores.”
- **What the notice tells consumers to do:**
  - “Consumers should stop using Ultimate Forza Male Supplement immediately.”
  - “Consumers may return unopened or partially used product to a GNC store for a full refund.”
  - “Consumers with questions may contact GNC Customer Service at 1-877-462-4700 or GNCRecalls@gnc-hq.com.”
  - “Consumers should contact their physician or healthcare provider if they have experienced any problems that may be related to taking or using this product.”
- **Announced:** company announcement October 24, 2026; posted by FDA October 24, 2026
- **Official notice:** [Tonight We Ride, LLC Issues Voluntary Recall of Ultimate Forza Male Supplement Due to Presence of Undeclared Sildenafil, Tadalafil, and Dapoxetine](https://www.fda.gov/safety/recalls-market-withdrawals-safety-alerts/tonight-we-ride-llc-issues-voluntary-recall-ultimate-forza-male-supplement-due-presence-undeclared)

Product details, reasons and instructions are quoted from the notice. openFDA was used only to cross-check, as [its documentation](https://open.fda.gov/apis/food/enforcement/) advises. If a source cannot be read, we say so above rather than report a quiet week.
