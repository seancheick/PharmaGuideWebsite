**PharmaGuide Weekly.** Dietary-supplement recalls FDA posted October 19, 2026 to October 25, 2026. Each recall covers only the products and lots in its notice, not every product with the same ingredient.

<Callout tone="info">
This roundup restates official FDA notices; it is not medical advice. If you have one of these products, check it against the card and follow the notice's instructions. A pharmacist or your clinician can help if you have taken it and have questions.
</Callout>

## New recalls

**Check your package**

| Package | Item number | UPC |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Ultimate Forza Multi-Capsule/Box | 810208 | 868592000002 |
| Ultimate Forza Single Pack | 810222 | 868592000040 |


The Multi-Capsule/Box configuration contains ten blue hard-shell capsules, and the Single Pack contains one capsule.

**Where it was sold:** These items were distributed through certain independently owned and operated GNC franchise locations in the United States from 2017 to October 1, 2026. The products were not sold through GNC corporate stores.

**Refunds, questions and health concerns (from the notice):**

- Consumers may return unopened or partially used product to a GNC store for a full refund.
- A receipt or the phone number or email address associated with the loyalty account used for the purchase should be provided so the purchase can be verified.
- Consumers with questions may contact GNC Customer Service at 1-877-462-4700 or GNCRecalls@gnc-hq.com.
- Consumers should contact their physician or healthcare provider if they have experienced any problems that may be related to taking or using this product.

</RecallCard>
