**PharmaGuide Weekly.** Dietary-supplement recalls FDA posted November 2, 2026 to November 8, 2026. Each recall covers only the products and lots in its notice, not every product with the same ingredient.

<Callout tone="info">
This roundup restates official FDA notices; it is not medical advice. If you have one of these products, check it against the card and follow the notice's instructions. A pharmacist or your clinician can help if you have taken it and have questions.
</Callout>

## Were there any supplement recalls this week?

No. We checked FDA's [recalls page](https://www.fda.gov/safety/recalls-market-withdrawals-safety-alerts) and the [Enforcement Report](https://www.fda.gov/safety/recalls-market-withdrawals-safety-alerts/enforcement-reports) for November 4, 2026: neither listed a new dietary-supplement recall, a supplement recall newly added to the Enforcement Report, or an update to an earlier one for Nov 2–8, 2026.

A recall is about specific products, not an ingredient. To see how PharmaGuide checks a product you already own, read [how it works](/how-it-works/) and [our methodology](/methodology/). More safety posts are on the [blog](/blog/).
