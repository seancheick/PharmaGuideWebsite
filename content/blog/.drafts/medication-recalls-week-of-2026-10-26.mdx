**PharmaGuide Weekly.** Medication recalls FDA posted October 26, 2026 to November 1, 2026. Each recall covers only the products and lots in its record, not every product with the same active ingredient.

<Callout tone="info">
**Check your lot first, and talk to your doctor or pharmacist before changing how you take a medicine.** [FDA's guidance](https://www.fda.gov/drugs/drug-recalls/understanding-drug-recalls-what-know-and-what-do): for class I recalls, the most serious, follow the recalling company's instructions. For class II and III, consumers can generally continue taking the medicine unless the recalling company provides other instructions, and in some instances stopping may be more harmful than continuing. This roundup restates official FDA records; it is not medical advice.
</Callout>
